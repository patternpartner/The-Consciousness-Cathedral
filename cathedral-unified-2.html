WHY 2.0 EXISTS

The old unified system had qualities no component has alone: it responded to its history, its verdicts emerged from parts in mutual influence, and it was one thing you could encounter. It was retired anyway, because its unity was also the disease — with everything wired to everything, its adaptation was indistinguishable from its corruption, and there was nothing fixed to compare it against. It had to be taken apart to even be assessed. Every part was then rebuilt with a certificate: eleven validation registers for the operational analyzer, six pre-registered runs for the relational one, a frozen red-team strike, gates committed to git before verdicts, failures published as failures.

2.0 is the reassembly with the learnings. The unity moved from shared state to shared shape — every load-bearing structure here is a condition bound to a response, committed before the event it governs — and the qualities January had are not deleted but re-homed at timescales where they can be trusted:

January 1.0 (silent)Unified 2.0 (under contract)
Memory fed judgment invisibly; devices drifted apart The notebook remembers everything and informs nothing; the no-feedback rule is test-enforced
Auto-calibration reweighted verdicts ±20% from hidden state Calibration is proposed with evidence, human-applied, append-only ledgered, stamped on verdicts
Adaptation happened at runtime, unobservably Adaptation happens across versions, in a public ledger — the git history is the developmental arc
Parts influenced each other inside one merged process Parts are composed on this page and isolated in measurement; relation itself became the subject matter of the second analyzer
Unity asserted by narrative Unity enacted by discipline; every claim links to its certificate in docs/

The boundaries carry their reopening conditions. Nothing here is accepted forever by default: the flagship's known soft boundary reopens at ≥ 10/20 shuffle retention on fresh data; the mandated-binding decision reopens on evidence of boilerplate earning structural verdicts; even the presumption about the assistant that built this page has a proposed gate in the session record. A boundary without a reopening condition is not a decision — it is a default nobody has to defend.

What this page will never do: let one analyzer read the other, let memory feed a verdict, recalibrate without the ledger, phone home, or emit a different verdict for the same input. If you want those properties changed, change them in the source where the change can be seen — that is the entire lesson, and the reason this file exists at all.
