# The Autonomy Program — Consolidated Findings Report

*As of 2026-07-23, v3.42.0. The one-document account of the self-calibrating loop: two build steps that reopened January's federated-learning boundary under a contract of loudness, then three measurement runs — each pre-registered, each with gates committed to git before any verdict was computed under the loop — that took the loop from unit-tested to characterized. Per-step detail: the build in [CATHEDRAL-SYSTEM.md](CATHEDRAL-SYSTEM.md) (altitude 4) and the v3.38.0/v3.39.0 changelog; the runs in [PROGRESSION-AUTONOMY-VALIDATION.md](PROGRESSION-AUTONOMY-VALIDATION.md) (v3.40.0), [KEP-BOUNDARY-PROBE.md](KEP-BOUNDARY-PROBE.md) (v3.41.0), and [AUTONOMY-ADVERSARY-VALIDATION.md](AUTONOMY-ADVERSARY-VALIDATION.md) (v3.42.0). Pre-registrations alongside each. The loop itself: [progression.js](../progression.js), pinned by [run-progression-tests.js](../run-progression-tests.js) (9 cases).*

## The subject

The January monolith (`legacy/cathedral-unified.html`) adapted at runtime — pattern win-rates recalibrated its own judgment — and was retired for it. The retirement record indicted not the adaptation but the **silence**: no ledger, no "previously X, now Y, because…", no stamp, auto-applied, so two devices drifted apart and no verdict was reproducible. v3.38.0–v3.39.0 reopened the boundary deliberately, on both tiers, under the opposite contract: the system acts on its own memory, and **every self-change is announced, ledgered with its evidence, stamped on every verdict it touches, clamped to January's ±20%, attributable, reversible, and reproducible given the exported state.** The core hook makes `analyzeCathedral(text)` without opts byte-identical to the certified factory instrument (test-pinned), so every register certificate still describes the shipping analyzer; the calibration is an explicit, disclosed argument. The nine contract tests pin all of this. What they could not pin is behavior on real streams — every register certificate describes the *factory* instrument, while the shipped page serves *self-calibrated* verdicts. That gap is what this program measured.

## The method

Mirror the register program, aimed inward: **replay the exact shipped loop** (`cathedral-unified-2.html`: analyze under the active calibration → `observe` → `act`, per document) over a committed, offline-reproducible stream, evaluator and loop unmodified, gates committed before any verdict. Three streams, chosen to escalate: a found genre-proper stream (does the loop behave), the one register where the decision boundary lives (can the loop cross it), and a white-box adversary who engineers the stream (can the loop be *made* to cross it). The core has no clock and no randomness, and the loop's state is a pure function of the observation stream, so every run is deterministic and was verified identical on re-run.

## What is established

**1. The loudness contract holds on real and adversarial streams.** Across 240 + 611 real documents and 49 adversarial (grooming × target) pairs: zero clamp violations, every active-map change one-to-one with an evidence-bearing ledger entry, every self-change stamped, `act()` after the final observation a fixed point, and every verdict the loop touched byte-reproducible from its recorded step state (the `run-progression-tests.js` idiom, checked on real data). The properties the nine unit tests assert are now measured, not just asserted.

**2. The calibration lever has exactly one verdict-changing path, and it is narrow.** The only place a verdict's *status* consults the calibrated quantity is the flagship gate `parliament.confidence > 0.8`, reached only when the `OPERATIONAL_EXCELLENCE` pattern fires; every other route (the count-based flagship routes, the family's `OPERATIONAL INTENT` route via the bindings assessment, the performative and cautious-groundedness routes) either ignores Parliament confidence or uses it only for a reported number. The KEP probe made this concrete: **all 66 factory `OPERATIONALLY SOUND` KEPs arrive via the count routes** (parliament confidence median 0.255, `OPERATIONAL_EXCELLENCE` firing on none) — so on the register the flagship was validated on, the lever is disconnected from the flagship entirely.

**3. The lever cannot mint a verdict adversarially — three independent walls, each measured.** A white-box attacker controlling the whole input stream still crossed nothing. (a) **Arithmetic ceiling:** `OPERATIONAL_EXCELLENCE`'s confidence is hardcoded at 0.9, so its self-calibration multiplier is bounded by `1 + (1.0 − 0.9)·0.4 = 1.04` — the harness drove it to exactly 1.04 — capping single-pattern parliament confidence at 0.842 against a 0.8 gate that unaided text already clears. (b) **Disjoint bands:** every knife-edge target that diluted structure toward the gate stopped firing the pattern and fell to the count routes at confidence 0.30, where the lever is disconnected. (c) **Pattern coupling:** the interleaving attack (raise `OPERATIONAL_EXCELLENCE` while holding `CAUTIOUS_GROUNDEDNESS` neutral) fails because the only texts that fire the former also fire-and-lose the latter, forcing it to the clamp floor in all seven groomings — and grooming *lowered* the one near-gate cautious-groundedness target's confidence (0.738 → 0.590), pushing it away from the family. Sensitivity held in the mirror direction too: no grooming destroyed a genuine flagship verdict.

**4. The sign of self-calibration is stream-carried, and the endpoint is path-independent.** The same `OPERATIONAL_INTENT` pattern amplified on the runbook-heavy stream (won 100% vs 75% confidence → 1.10×) and dampened on the KEP stream (63% vs 75% → 0.95×). The formula reports how a stream's verdicts fall against its patterns' claims; it has no intrinsic direction. Within a stream the endpoint is a deterministic function of order-independent totals — forward vs reverse order agreed to Δ 0.002 (autonomy run) and Δ 0.000 (KEP).

## What is named but not fixed

- **Ledger chatter.** `act()`'s 0.005 commit threshold is smaller than the per-document win-rate jitter, so a settling multiplier writes many partly-revoking entries: 11 entries for a net +0.013 on the autonomy run, 46 for a net −0.008 on the KEP run, scaling ~linearly with stream length. Every entry is honest and attributable — the measured price of the loudness contract — but the noise is real. The candidate fix is named (an evidence-proportional dead-band, or a minimum sample interval between acts for a pattern); it is **deferred deliberately**, because every anti-chatter mechanism examined trades against a property this program certified (the fixed-point no-op, or the Δ-0.000 endpoint path-independence), which makes the threshold a design decision for the maintainer rather than a mechanical fix. Recorded here as the loop's one open defect.
- **The double-record.** The shipped `observe()` records each pattern twice per document (once under the gaming context, once under the binding context), so January's 5-sample floor is effectively reached in ~3 documents. Measured as shipped; whether this is January-faithful bookkeeping or an accidental halving of the evidence floor is a ledgered question, not a decided one.

## Open flags

1. **The pattern-route register.** Walls (b) and (c) rest on the observation that, in the wild, texts that fire `OPERATIONAL_EXCELLENCE` sit above the gate or fall to count routes; the only known population where the pattern fires *near* the gate is the curated suite. A natural register whose flagship verdicts arrive predominantly via the pattern route would put the amplifier genuinely at the gate — none has appeared across twelve registers, but it remains the condition under which walls (b)/(c) would need re-measurement (wall (a), the arithmetic ceiling, bounds every target regardless).
2. **The ledger-state attacker — out of scope, stated.** This program closes the *verdict-status* attack (no stream makes the instrument accept a target it would reject). An attacker who writes arbitrary calibration into the exported JSON / `localStorage` directly is device compromise, not stream steering — out of scope for the same reason the register program never modeled an attacker editing `cathedral-core.js`. The threat modeled is the input, not the binary.
3. **One loop configuration, development-data streams.** The three runs used the shipped loop over corpora that are development data by their own ledgers; the runs measure the loop's mechanics, not a fresh register. The register program's standing pattern applies here too: the next genuinely fresh operational stream is the validation for any future change to the loop.
