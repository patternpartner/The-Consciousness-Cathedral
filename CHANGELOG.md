## 3.8.0 — 2026-07-08

The real line is **silent change corrupts; ledgered change calibrates.** Contract: the notebook *proposes* recalibration when evidence shows a default threshold stopped discriminating on your data (plain-words evidence attached); only a person applies it; every change is an append-only ledger entry; every verdict is stamped with the calibration that produced it; reset is one tap and itself recorded. ## 3.11.0 — 2026-07-11
- **Binding-based substrate check** — the top salvage item from `docs/JANUARY-SALVAGE.md`, the January build teaching the tested core its own philosophy (binding over counting). `GamingDetector.checkSubstrateBinding` classifies each substrate term as bound or unbound by whether its sentence makes a structural claim (conditional, causal, counterfactual, threshold, comparative; ≥6 words), with a binding capacity of 2 terms per claim so one "if … then" can't launder a pile of vocabulary. ## 3.12.0 — 2026-07-11
